## A) DIAGNOSIS

### Top 10 Reasons brinkdesign.co Isn't Converting or Ranking

#### LOCAL SEO (3 Issues)
1. **No city-specific service pages** — Missing pages like `/security-cameras-rapid-city`, `/business-wifi-box-elder`. Google can't match you to "security cameras Rapid City" without dedicated pages.
2. **Weak service area targeting** — State-level pages exist (SD, WY, NE) but no individual city pages for Box Elder, Spearfish, or Sturgis. These are high-intent local searches going to competitors.
3. **LocalBusiness schema is basic** — No `priceRange`, `serviceArea` with cities, `openingHours`, or `hasOfferCatalog`. Google uses this for local pack rankings.

#### ON-PAGE SEO (3 Issues)
4. **Service page titles lack geo-modifiers** — Current: "Security Cameras – No Monthly Fees | Brink Design Co." Better: "Security Cameras Rapid City | No Monthly Fees | Brink Design"
5. **H1s don't target search intent** — Current hero H1: "Cameras, Wi-Fi & AV — Installed Right" — doesn't mention Rapid City. High-intent searches include location.
6. **Missing FAQ schema on service pages** — You have FAQ content but no FAQPage schema markup. This cuts you out of featured snippets.

#### TECHNICAL SEO (2 Issues)
7. **GTM placeholder not configured** — layout.tsx shows `GTM-XXXXXXX`. No actual tracking is firing. You cannot measure what you cannot track.
8. **No canonical on service pages missing alternates** — Some pages have canonicals, others don't. Mixed signals to Google.

#### CONTENT (1 Issue)
9. **5 case studies but none highlight local proof** — Case study titles mention Gillette Church, but no Rapid City commercial projects. Commercial is your revenue focus — show it.

#### CONVERSION (1 Issue)
10. **CTA is generic "Book a Site Visit"** — Fine, but contact form is generic (name, email, subject, message). No phone field, no property type, no timeline. Qualify leads better. Also, no sticky CTA on scroll.
